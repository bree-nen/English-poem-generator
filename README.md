<h1>🌷PoemNest</h1>

<p>PoemNest is a creative web application designed to give words a place to breathe.
The app allows users to explore, create, and enjoy poetry through a simple and welcoming interface.
It combines creativity with modern web development to create a space where users can turn thoughts, feelings, and ideas into poems.</p>

<h2>✨ Features</h2>
<br>
🎀 Create Poems — Write and express your own poetry.
<br>
🎀 Explore Poetry — Discover poems and creative writing.
<br>
🎀 Creative Expression — Turn ideas, emotions, and experiences into words.
<br>
🎀 Clean & Modern Interface — Designed to keep the focus on the poetry.
<br>
🎀 Responsive Design — Works across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
<br>
🎀 Interactive Experience — Smooth interactions designed to make creating and exploring poetry enjoyable.
<br>

<h2>🌸 About the Project</h2>
<br>
<p>PoemNest was created as a space where technology and creativity meet.
The idea behind the project is simple: everyone has a story, a feeling, or a thought that can become a poem.
The application demonstrates practical front-end development skills while exploring how technology can be used to encourage creativity and self-expression.</p>
<br>

<h2>💘 Project Goals</h2>
<br>
The main goals of Poem Nest are to:
<br>
💌Build an engaging and accessible poetry platform.
<br>
💌Practise modern front-end development.
<br>
💌Work with APIs and dynamic content.
<br>
💌Create responsive user interfaces.
<br>
💌Explore the intersection between technology and creative expression.
